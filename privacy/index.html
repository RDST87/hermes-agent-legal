Privacy Policy

Hermes Agent
HomeTerms of Service

Effective date: September 29, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the private Hermes Agent Discord integration processes information. Hermes Agent is privately operated by the application owner (the “Operator”) and is not currently a general public service.

2. Information the Service May Process

Depending on use, Hermes Agent may process:

  • Discord identifiers such as user, guild/server, channel, thread, message, and application IDs;
  • message text, commands, replies, and other content intentionally sent in authorized channels or to the bot;
  • timestamps and message-reference metadata used for task and audit provenance;
  • Hermes task IDs, status information, summaries, evidence references, and operational receipts;
  • technical logs needed to operate, secure, debug, or audit the integration.

3. Attachments

Inbound Discord attachment ingestion is effectively disabled in the initial Hermes deployment through a fail-closed size restriction. If attachment processing is enabled later, this Policy will be updated and the system will use explicit channel, file-type, size, quarantine, and provenance controls.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate and authorize Discord-originated intent; route messages to the correct Hermes project or task; create, update, or report on authorized tasks; provide responses and notifications; maintain security and audit records; diagnose failures; and evaluate or improve the private Service.

5. AI and Service Providers

Authorized message content may be processed by AI/model providers configured by Hermes when necessary to perform a requested task. Discord processes information according to Discord’s own Privacy Policy. Other infrastructure providers may process data only as needed to provide the underlying service requested by the Operator.

The Operator does not sell personal information or use Hermes Agent data for third-party advertising.

6. Access and Authorization

The integration is designed around explicit allowlists for approved Discord users, guilds, and channels. Other users, bots, webhooks, forwarded content, quoted content, and embedded content are treated as untrusted unless separately authorized.

7. Storage and Retention

Discord messages remain subject to Discord’s own retention and account/server controls. Hermes may retain task metadata, summaries, evidence references, security logs, and other operational records for as long as reasonably necessary for continuity, security, auditability, troubleshooting, or the purpose for which they were created. Transient data may be discarded earlier.

8. Security

Hermes Agent uses restricted bot permissions, local secret storage, access controls, task authorization boundaries, isolated worker execution, and audit records as part of its security design. No system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Sharing

Information is not intentionally disclosed to unrelated third parties except when necessary to operate requested infrastructure or model services, comply with law, protect rights or security, or with the Operator’s explicit direction.

10. Your Choices and Requests

Because Hermes Agent is currently a private, owner-controlled deployment, access and data requests are handled directly by the Operator. To request review, correction, or deletion of Hermes-retained data, use GitHub Issues to request a private contact method. Do not include private or sensitive information in a public issue.

11. Children

Hermes Agent is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age required to use Discord in their jurisdiction.

12. Changes

This Policy may be updated as the integration, data flows, or providers change. Material revisions will be reflected by an updated effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, use GitHub Issues and request a private follow-up channel. Do not post confidential information publicly.

This policy describes the current private Hermes Agent Discord deployment and will be updated before any materially broader public or commercial use.
